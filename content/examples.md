# Examples

The following are three famous public domain short stories. Beneath the summary
for each section is a link to the report.

## The Cask of Amontillado by Edgar Allan Poe

In "The Cask of Amontillado," the narrator, Montresor, seeks revenge on
Fortunato for a past insult. Montresor lures Fortunato into the catacombs under
his palazzo with the promise of a rare cask of Amontillado wine, only to then
chain him to a wall and seal him in alive.

[View the manuscript report for The Cask of Amontillado](/report/fsMXGvHS8g).

## The Signal-Man by Charles Dickens

In "The Signal-Man" by Charles Dickens, a railway signal-man tells the narrator
about mysterious and supernatural incidents he has experienced at his post,
including ghostly appearances and a foreboding bell ring that seem to predict
fatal accidents on the line. The story ends with the signal-man's own death,
which tragically fulfills the ghostly warnings.

[View the manuscript report for The Signal-Man](/report/Zg9dYkSBWA).

## Mrs. Dalloway in Bond Street by Virginia Woolf

"Mrs. Dalloway in Bond Street" is a short story by Virginia Woolf. In it,
Clarissa Dalloway embarks on a simple errand to buy gloves, but her thoughts
meander through memories, social observations, and reflections on life, death,
and the changes wrought by World War I.

[View the manuscript report for Mrs. Dalloway in Bond Street](/report/wBHkknwVQL).
