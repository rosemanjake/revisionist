# Frequently Asked Questions

- **What types of manuscripts does Revisionist accept?** Revisionist accepts all
  fiction manuscripts, from 150 - 100,000 words. However, you should not send
  Revisionist collections of short stories.

- **What file formats can I submit?** We accept manuscripts in PDF, DOCX, TXT,
  and EPUB files. Please ensure your manuscript is properly formatted and free
  of any password protection. You can also just paste in the text directly.

- **What happens if I close the window while Revisionist is processing my
  manuscript?** We will email you a link to the final report, so you can't lose
  it. Make sure you input the correct email.

- **How long does it take to receive my review?** As we use the latest AI models
  to produce our analysis, you can expect to receive your report in just a few
  minutes.

- **How detailed are the reviews?** Our reviews are comprehensive and cover
  various aspects of your manuscript, including plot structure, character
  development, pacing, writing style, and more. You'll receive an overall
  assessment as well as specific suggestions for improvement.

- **Can I submit multiple manuscripts at once?** No. Revisionist processes
  manuscripts one at a time. If you would like reports for several manuscripts,
  you will need to purchase a license for each.

- **Is my manuscript secure?** Absolutely. We take the security and
  confidentiality of your manuscript very seriously. We do not store the
  manuscript itself, only the review.

- **How much does Revisionist cost?** There is a flat fee of $5 USD for each
  license.

- **Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with my review?** We strive to
  provide high-quality, valuable feedback to all our clients. However, if you're
  not satisfied with your review, please contact our support team at
  [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}) and we'll work with you to resolve
  any issues.

- **How do I sign up for Revisionist?** No sign up is necessary. Simply buy a
  license key, upload your file, and get your report. We'll email you a link.

- **What does the report look like?** For several examples of the reports the
  Revisionist produces, see our [examples](/examples).

- **I have more questions. How can I get in touch?** If you have any additional
  questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team
  at [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}). We're always happy to help!

- **What are the terms and conditions?** Please refer to the
  [terms and conditions](/terms) for more information.
