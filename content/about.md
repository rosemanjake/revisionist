# About Revisionist

Revolutionise your writing with Revisionist, the AI-powered manuscript review
service. Submit your fiction manuscript and receive comprehensive, constructive
feedback in no time.

## How It Works

Using Revisionist is simple:

1. Upload your manuscript
2. AI analyses your work
3. Receive detailed review
4. Revise and improve

## Why Revisionist?

- **Cutting-edge AI technology**: Our advanced AI models provide high-quality,
  accurate, and relevant feedback on your work.
- **Comprehensive analysis**: We cover all essential aspects of your manuscript,
  including plot, characters, pacing, and writing style.
- **Quick turnaround**: Get your detailed review fast, so you can make timely
  revisions and keep your writing projects on track.
- **Competitive pricing**: We charge a small flat fee of $5 USD for each
  manuscript. This is an enormous reduction in price compared to traditional
  manuscript reviews.
- **Secure and confidential**: We prioritise the security of your manuscripts
  and protect your intellectual property. We do not store the manuscript itself,
  only the review.
