# Privacy Policy

At Revisionist, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our
users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your
personal information when you use our web app for reviewing fiction manuscripts.
By using our service, you consent to the practices described in this policy,
which is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information: When you use our web app, we collect your email
address, which you voluntarily provide to us. We store this email address along
with the reviews you submit to our platform.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

a. Providing Our Service: We use your email address to send you a link to the
report containing the review of your submitted manuscript. b. Communication: We
may use your email address to send you important notifications, updates, and
information related to our service.

## 3. Data Retention

We retain your email address and associated reviews for as long as necessary to
fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention
period is required or permitted by law.

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your
personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or
destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the
internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights: a. Right to Access: You have the
right to request access to your personal information that we hold. b. Right to
Rectification: You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate
personal information. c. Right to Erasure: You have the right to request the
deletion of your personal information, subject to certain limitations. d. Right
to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request the restriction of
processing of your personal information. e. Right to Data Portability: You have
the right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used,
and machine-readable format and to transmit that information to another
controller. f. Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing
of your personal information for certain purposes. g. Right to Lodge a
Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority
if you believe that our processing of your personal information violates the
GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information
provided below.

changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last
