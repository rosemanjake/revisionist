# Overview

Briefly summarize the key elements of the manuscript, including the main plot
points, central characters, and overarching themes. This synopsis should provide
context for the suggestions and feedback in the rest of the report.

## Overall Impression

- **Initial Thoughts:** Share your initial reactions and impressions of the
  story, characters, and writing style. Highlight areas that show promise and
  those that may need improvement.
- **Originality:** Comment on the manuscript's uniqueness within its genre.
  Suggest ways to make the story stand out and feel fresh.
- **Emotional Impact:** Describe the emotional resonance of the story. Suggest
  ways to strengthen the emotional connection with readers.

## Suggestions for Improvement

For each section below (Plot, Characters, Prose, Theme), provide a summary of
the key strengths and areas for improvement. Offer specific, actionable
suggestions and strategies to address the identified issues and build upon the
existing strengths of the manuscript. Use examples from the text to illustrate
your points. Keep it simple at this point as this is still in the overview.

# Plot

- _Overview_: Provide an overview of your thoughts on the plot and how it could
  be improved.
- **Pacing:** Identify areas where the pacing could be improved. Offer
  suggestions for tightening or expanding specific sections to maintain reader
  engagement.
- **Structure:** Evaluate the plot structure and suggest modifications to
  enhance the story's impact and effectiveness.
- **Engagement:** Pinpoint moments where reader interest might wane and provide
  ideas for increasing engagement and stakes.
- **Key Elements:** Offer suggestions for refining the exposition, conflict,
  rising action, climax, and resolution to maximize their impact on the reader.
- **Subplots & Integration:** Propose ways to better integrate subplots into the
  main storyline. Suggest strategies for using foreshadowing effectively and
  resolving any inconsistencies or plot holes.

# Characters

- _Overview_: Provide an overview of your thoughts on the characters and how
  they could be improved.
- **Depth and Complexity:** Suggest ways to make the characters more
  well-rounded, believable, and unique. Provide examples of how to add depth to
  their traits, motivations, and flaws.
- **Consistency:** Point out any inconsistencies in character actions or
  dialogue and provide ideas for improvement.
- **Major Characters:** For main characters, suggest ways to strengthen their
  motivations, conflicts, and arcs. Offer ideas for making their transformations
  more believable and impactful.
- **Relationships & Dynamics:** Propose ways to enhance the chemistry between
  characters and make their dialogue more natural. Suggest how relationships
  could better influence character development.
- **Role in Plot and Theme:** Offer suggestions for better integrating
  characters into the plot and theme. Identify opportunities to strengthen these
  connections.

# Prose

- _Overview_: Provide an overview of your thoughts on the prose and how it could
  be improved.
- **Voice:** Provide suggestions for making the authorial voice more distinct,
  compelling, and appropriate for the genre/audience.
- **Tone:** Offer ideas for ensuring the overall mood is consistent and suitable
  for the story's themes/genre.
- **Mechanics:** Identify frequent grammar, punctuation, spelling, and
  formatting errors. Offer correction examples and strategies for improvement.
- **Effectiveness:** Suggest ways to better communicate ideas, evoke emotions,
  immerse the reader, and create memorable moments through the prose.

# Theme

- _Overview_: Provide an overview of your thoughts on the themes and how they
  could be improved.
- **Identification:** Clarify the central themes and subthemes if needed.
- **Development:** Offer suggestions for conveying the themes with more nuance
  and complexity. Provide ideas for expressing themes through characters and
  plot.
- **Effectiveness & Impact:** Suggest ways to make the themes more
  thought-provoking, emotionally resonant, and relatable to a wide audience.
- **Presentation:** Offer ideas for exploring the themes with more depth and
  subtlety. Suggest ways to avoid heavy-handedness or inconsistency in thematic
  presentation.
